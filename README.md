# Astra City

A tiny living city in one HTML file: procedural low-poly neighborhoods, lane-following traffic, working signals, and a gentle day–night cycle. Watch windows and streetlights glow after sunset, or select a building to discover its story.

## Visit

Open **index.html** in a modern Chrome or Firefox browser, or visit the live GitHub Pages URL once published: **https://fatihlabsai.github.io/astra-city/**

An internet connection is required to load Three.js and OrbitControls from the CDN. Everything else—geometry, styling, and simulation—is contained in `index.html`. No build step is needed.

## Controls

- **Drag:** orbit. **Right-drag:** pan. **Scroll:** zoom.
- **Touch:** one finger to orbit; two fingers to pan or pinch to zoom.
- **Click a building:** see its generated name, floor count, and type. Click empty space or × to close.
- **Passage of time:** pause at 0× or accelerate up to 5×; affects both traffic and the clock.
- **Regenerate City:** create a fresh seeded layout while keeping your camera and time of day.

A full day takes about 6 minutes 40 seconds at 1×. Traffic uses right-hand lanes and coordinated green, yellow, and all-red clearance phases. Instanced buildings, windows, cars, and street furniture keep rendering economical.
